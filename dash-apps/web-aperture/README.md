# Aperture CAN inputs

DashPilot decodes the signals and supplies them to Aperture. CH and VEH refer to the Talas9 chassis and vehicle exports. The bundled Juniper decoder also contains frames identified in DashKit captures. A DBC entry describes the frame format, but a vehicle still has to broadcast that frame on a bus DashKit can see.

## Driving and display

- **Speed and gear.** The DashKit/Juniper decoder uses `DI_uiSpeed` and `DI_uiSpeedUnits` (0x257), and `DI_gear` (0x118, in the Talas9 VEH export). The Talas9 exports do not include 0x257. `UI_distanceUnits` (0x293) selects the large speedometer unit. An alternate speed reading appears in map units when they differ.
- **Steering and lane visualization.** `SCCM_steeringAngle` (0x129) steers the lane preview and rotates the active Autosteer icon. Speed controls the motion of the lane markings. The preview geometry is generated in Aperture.
- **Posted speed limit.** `UI_mppSpeedLimit` and `UI_mapSpeedLimitUnits` (Talas9 CH 0x2F8, VEH 0x373) provide the map number and unit. `DAS_fusedSpeedLimit` (0x399 CH) is the fallback. `GTW_mapRegion` (0x7FF VEH, mux 3) selects the European or North American sign family. A live Canadian override needs a verified country mapping, for example from `GTW_country` (mux 1).
- **Autopilot and FSD.** `DAS_autopilotState` (0x399 CH) distinguishes available, active Autosteer, Navigate on Autopilot, active FSD, and aborting. `UI_enableFullSelfDriving` (0x3FD, mux 0) indicates the selected FSD setting, and `UI_autopilotDrivingProfile` (mux 2) gives the profile name. The Juniper decoder reads the cruise set speed from `DI_digitalSpeed` (0x286). That signal is absent from these Talas9 exports. `DAS_accSpeedLimit` describes a map-derived ACC limit.
- **Driver warnings.** `DAS_autopilotHandsOnState` (0x399 CH) gives the visual or escalated alert level. `DAS_LC_handsOnReason` (0x5D9 CH, mux 2) distinguishes supported steering and attention prompts. `DAS_w042_autopilotAborting` (0x32A CH) also triggers the takeover display.
- **Battery and power.** `BMS_socUI` and `BMS_enoughEnergyForConvenienceFe` (0x292 VEH) provide percentage and the convenience-energy flag. If SOC is unavailable, the app estimates it from `BMS_nominalEnergyRemaining`, `BMS_nominalFullPackEnergy`, and `BMS_energyBuffer` (0x352 VEH). `BMS_packVoltage` and `BMS_packCurrent` (0x132 VEH) give the power estimate. `BMS_maxDischargePower` and `BMS_maxRegenPower` (0x252 VEH) give the current limits. The striped unavailable fraction requires nominal power references for the vehicle. The snowflake still needs an identified OEM display trigger.
- **Appearance and temperature.** `GTW_exteriorColor` (0x7FF VEH, mux 2) selects the car paint. `UI_displayInDarkMode` (0x3DF VEH) selects the theme with the app theme as a fallback. `VCFRONT_tempAmbientFiltered` (0x321 VEH) provides ambient temperature. Temperature units and the clock come from the app.

## Body and warning indicators

- **Turn signals and hazards.** `VCLEFT_turnSignalStatus` (0x3E2 VEH) and `VCRIGHT_turnSignalStatus` (0x3E3 VEH). Hazards illuminate both arrows.
- **Closures and restraints.** `VCLEFT_frontLatchStatus` and `VCLEFT_rearLatchStatus` (0x102 VEH), `VCRIGHT_frontLatchStatus`, `VCRIGHT_rearLatchStatus`, and `VCRIGHT_trunkLatchStatus` (0x103 VEH), `VCFRONT_frunkLatchStatus` and `VCFRONT_anyDoorOpen` (0x2E1 VEH, mux 0), `VCLEFT_liftgatePosition` (0x142 VEH, mux 1), and `VCLEFT_frontBuckleSwitch` (0x3C2 VEH, mux 0). The live closure overlay uses open/closed state. Detailed latch states, vehicle locking (`VCSEC_vehicleLockStatus`), and charge-port door position (`CP_chargeDoorOpenUI`, 0x25D VEH) need separate app mapping.
- **Lighting and chassis telltales.** `VCFRONT_lightingCoreState`, `VCFRONT_highBeamControlState`, `VCFRONT_latchedHighBeamsFunction`, and `VCFRONT_frontFogFunctionState` (0x3F5 VEH) cover lighting. `DI_vehicleHoldTelltaleOn`, `DI_vdcTelltaleOn/Flash`, and `DI_tcTelltaleOn/Flash` (0x2B6 VEH) cover Hold, stability, and traction. `RCM_warningIndicatorState` (0x211 CH), `TPMS_c_globalHardWarning` and `TPMS_systemMalfunction` (0x36F CH), and `ESP_a102_absFault` (0x3D5 CH, mux 1) cover airbag, tire, and ABS warnings. Parking-brake and brake-system variants, stability-off, reduced power, cold battery, and limited-regeneration icons still need live mappings.

## Charging

- **Charge mode and connection.** `CP_powerTransferStatus` and `CP_evseChargeType` (0x13D VEH) drive the AC/DC charging screen.
- **AC.** `PCS_chgInputVoltage`, `PCS_chgLineCurrent`, and `PCS_chgInputPower` (0x264 VEH), `CP_evseAcCurrentLimit` (0x13D VEH), and `CC_numPhases` (0x31C VEH).
- **DC.** `BMS_batteryInputPower` (0x212 VEH). The DC screen shows kW and omits voltage and current.
- **Time, energy, and range.** `UI_chargeTimeUntilTerminationPct` (0x33B VEH) gives minutes remaining. Aperture computes miles added from the difference in `UI_ratedRange` (0x33A VEH). It computes kWh added from the difference in `BMS_acChargerKwhTotal` or `BMS_dcChargerKwhTotal` (0x3F2 VEH, mux 0 or 1). Both deltas start with the first valid reading observed while charging, so opening the app mid-session shows the gain since then. The live feed has no confirmed instantaneous range-added-per-hour signal.
